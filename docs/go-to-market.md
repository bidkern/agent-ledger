# Go-To-Market Plan

## Positioning

Prooflane is not "AI for compliance."

Prooflane is:

- the fastest way to answer customer security questionnaires
- a controlled trust-evidence workflow
- a revenue accelerator for companies stuck in procurement

Core promise:

"Close security reviews in hours, not weeks."

## Who to sell first

Priority segments:

1. AI startups already being asked about data handling, model retention, and subprocessor controls.
2. Developer-tool and infrastructure vendors with growing enterprise pipelines.
3. SaaS companies that already have SOC 2 but still answer questionnaires manually.

Reject these early:

- companies with no enterprise motion
- companies without a baseline security owner
- teams that need full compliance automation instead of assurance workflow acceleration

## Pilot offer

Sell this first:

- 30-day pilot
- $5k setup + $2.5k monthly
- up to 2 active questionnaires at once
- 48-hour first-draft turnaround
- human-reviewed answer pack

Why it works:

- low enough friction to close quickly
- high enough price to qualify seriousness
- lets us learn without locking into underpriced annual contracts

## Conversion path

After the pilot:

- Core plan at $24k ARR for teams with steady questionnaire flow
- Scale plan at $48k ARR for multi-team usage and advanced evidence handling
- Enterprise plan for regulated or high-volume organizations

Conversion triggers:

- more than 3 questionnaires per quarter
- sales team depends on faster turnaround
- customer success or security team asks for reusable evidence workflows

## Outbound motion

Channels:

- founder-led email
- LinkedIn outreach
- warm intros from investors, operators, and auditors
- partnerships with fractional CISOs and managed compliance firms

Best hook:

"We help your team answer security questionnaires and AI governance forms in 48 hours using your approved trust content, with human review before anything is sent."

## Demo structure

15-minute demo flow:

1. Show the questionnaire arriving.
2. Show answer generation against approved source material.
3. Show red flags and confidence gaps.
4. Show controlled evidence packaging.
5. Show how approved answers compound into future speed.

## Pipeline math

A simple, focused model:

- 200 target accounts
- 10% reply or intro rate
- 20 serious conversations
- 6 pilots
- 4 annual conversions

Repeat quarterly and improve.

With $24k-$48k ARR contracts, this becomes a real business quickly without needing massive user volume.
