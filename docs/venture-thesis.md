# Prooflane Venture Thesis

## One-sentence thesis

Build an AI-native customer assurance copilot for B2B software companies that need to answer security questionnaires, AI governance forms, and procurement packets faster than their team can do manually.

## Why this is the fastest credible path to $1M

This market has four traits that matter more than hype:

1. The pain is tied directly to closed-won revenue.
2. Buyers already budget for trust, compliance, and vendor review tools.
3. The work is repetitive enough for AI to draft meaningful first passes.
4. Human review can stay in the loop while the answer corpus compounds.

That combination means we can sell early with a concierge layer, gather real workflow data, and grow into software instead of waiting for a perfect autonomous product.

## Product definition

Prooflane ingests approved trust content:

- security policies
- SOC 2 summaries
- prior questionnaires
- architecture notes
- AI governance statements
- subprocessors and data-flow notes

It turns new questionnaires into:

- review-ready first drafts
- evidence mapping suggestions
- controlled document bundles
- red-flag lists for legal, security, and platform owners
- reusable approved answers that improve future drafts

## Ideal customer profile

- B2B software company
- 20 to 500 employees
- already selling into security-conscious mid-market or enterprise buyers
- receives recurring security reviews or vendor assessments
- has some baseline trust artifacts, but answering questionnaires is still slow and scattered

Best first wedge:

- AI infrastructure vendors
- developer tools companies
- data platforms
- fintech and healthtech vendors with strong deal friction

## Why now

Market signals with concrete dates:

- On February 12, 2025, Drata acquired SafeBase for $250M, showing strategic value in trust-center and security-review workflows.
- On March 25, 2025, Drata said it had reached $100M ARR and 7,000 customers, and highlighted leadership in vendor security and privacy assessment.
- On July 23, 2025, Vanta announced a $150M Series D at a $4.15B valuation and said it serves 12,000+ companies.
- Drata's AI Questionnaire Assistance has publicly claimed more than 1 million questions processed and 28,700+ hours saved, validating both repetition and demand for AI-assisted answers.

Inference:

The category is real, the budgets are real, and strategic buyers are already consolidating around customer assurance workflows. The opening is not invent trust management. The opening is to build a faster, narrower, more opinionated execution layer for teams that need results immediately.

## Business model

Recommended pricing:

- Pilot Sprint: $5,000 setup + $2,500 per month
- Core: $24,000 ARR
- Scale: $48,000 ARR
- Enterprise AI Assurance: $90,000+ ARR for multi-workspace or heavily regulated teams

Path to $1M ARR:

- 42 customers at $24k ARR
- 21 customers at $48k ARR
- or a blended base of 10 scale accounts plus 20 core accounts

This is why we should avoid low-ACV horizontal AI products. We want a small customer count with meaningful revenue per logo.

## Competitive angle

We should not try to out-suite Vanta or Drata on day one.

We should win on:

- faster implementation
- a sharper questionnaire-only wedge
- founder-led white glove service
- AI governance and customer assurance depth
- security posture that makes buyers comfortable sharing sensitive docs

## AI-made positioning

The honest claim is:

"Prooflane is an AI-made, AI-operated company with human ownership and human sign-off for anything customer-facing."

That language is strong enough to be differentiated and honest enough to survive diligence.

## Million-dollar acquisition logic

The likely first million dollars of enterprise value will come from revenue and strategic fit, not from vanity users.

Strategic buyer types:

- trust management platforms
- GRC vendors
- trust center providers
- identity or secure-collaboration platforms
- PE-backed compliance rollups

What makes the asset interesting:

- sticky workflow embedded in sales and security teams
- a structured corpus of approved answers and evidence mappings
- strong gross margins once human review narrows to exception handling
- trusted brand in a high-friction buying moment
