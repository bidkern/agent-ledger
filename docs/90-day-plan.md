# 90-Day Plan

## Goal

Launch Prooflane from zero to a secure pilot-ready business with enough traction to support a path to $50k-$70k MRR by day 90 and a realistic $1M ARR path within the following 6-12 months.

## Day 0-14: Build the wedge

Outputs:

- working landing page and demo shell
- pricing and offer structure
- secure intake narrative and architecture
- 200-account target list
- cold outbound copy, demo deck, and founder FAQ

Targets:

- 50 outbound messages sent
- 10 discovery conversations booked
- 3 design partners in conversation

Product priorities:

- questionnaire upload intake
- draft answer generation
- answer confidence scoring
- evidence reference suggestions
- manual reviewer notes

## Day 15-30: Sell pilots before polishing

Outputs:

- first three pilot customers signed
- first real questionnaires processed
- approved answer library structure defined
- onboarding checklist and service SOP created

Targets:

- 3 pilot customers
- $15k-$25k booked cash including setup fees
- 25+ real questionnaires ingested
- 80% of questions categorized into reusable answer families

Execution rule:

Do not over-automate edge cases. Human review is allowed. The objective is speed, learning, and revenue.

## Day 31-60: Standardize what repeats

Outputs:

- stable answer taxonomy
- evidence freshness owners
- AI governance response pack
- legal-review and security-review exception queue
- revised pricing based on observed workload

Targets:

- 8 paying customers
- $25k-$40k MRR
- 70% first-draft completion on recurring question clusters
- average turnaround below 48 hours

## Day 61-90: Convert pilots into durable revenue

Outputs:

- annual contract packaging
- customer case studies
- channel outreach to fractional CISOs, compliance consultants, and audit partners
- customer assurance room concept for controlled sharing
- first contractor or analyst for review operations

Targets:

- 15 paying customers
- $50k-$70k MRR
- 2 case studies
- 1 channel partner
- 1 repeatable outbound playbook

## Weekly cadence

Every week:

- Monday: pipeline review and pricing objections
- Tuesday: product improvements from live questionnaire friction
- Wednesday: outbound and demos
- Thursday: customer delivery and corpus cleanup
- Friday: metrics, security review, and SOP updates

## What success looks like after 90 days

- customers use Prooflane during active deals
- answers are getting better because the corpus is compounding
- security reviewers trust the output because humans still approve it
- the company has enough ACV and repeatable pain to justify focused scale instead of random idea-hopping
