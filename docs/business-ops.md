# Business Operations

## Company setup

Recommended structure:

- Delaware C corp
- standard founder IP assignment
- contractor invention assignment agreements
- standard MSA + DPA + security addendum

## First tooling stack

- Product: Next.js app, Postgres, private object storage
- Auth: WorkOS or Auth0 before production
- CRM: HubSpot or Attio
- Ticketing: Linear
- Support and incidents: Slack + PagerDuty or Opsgenie
- Monitoring: Sentry + infrastructure logs
- Knowledge: Notion or internal docs repo

## Core KPIs

Track weekly:

- new questionnaires ingested
- time to first draft
- human review minutes per questionnaire
- pilot-to-annual conversion rate
- MRR and ACV
- gross margin after review labor
- evidence freshness coverage

## Hiring sequence

Do not hire broadly early.

Hire in this order:

1. part-time compliance/security analyst
2. implementation or customer assurance operator
3. first full-time engineer after repeatable pilot signal

## Budget shape

Spend early on:

- secure infrastructure
- outbound and founder-led sales
- legal and insurance
- analyst support for human review

Avoid early spend on:

- large paid ads
- broad PLG infrastructure
- oversized internal tooling

## Operating principle

The company should behave like a high-trust, high-velocity boutique system.
Everything unnecessary gets cut.
Everything customer-facing must feel reliable, sober, and security-conscious.
